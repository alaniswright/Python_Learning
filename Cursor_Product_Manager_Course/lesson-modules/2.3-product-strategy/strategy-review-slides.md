## Slide 12: Free AI Drives Adoption, Not Revenue

**Action Title:** Free AI Drives Adoption, Not Revenue

**Content:**
- **Pricing Model:** Include AI free in all plans (like Linear)
- **Rationale:** Company priority is activation/retention, not AI revenue
- **Usage Limits:** 100 AI actions/user/month free, paid tier for heavy usage
- **Cost Control:** Monitor API costs weekly, target <$2/user/month

**Visual:** Pricing comparison (TaskFlow free vs. Notion $20/user/month)

**Takeaway:** Removing pricing friction accelerates adoption

---

## Slide 13: Top Risks and Our Mitigations

**Action Title:** Top Risks and Our Mitigations

**Content:**
- **Risk 1:** Voice features don't drive adoption
  - *Mitigation:* Build keyboard fallback, A/B test, pivot if <30% usage
- **Risk 2:** OpenAI API costs explode
  - *Mitigation:* Usage limits, monitor weekly, cache responses
- **Risk 3:** Competitors copy voice features
  - *Mitigation:* 12-18 month head start, focus on integration depth
- **Risk 4:** Small teams don't want voice
  - *Mitigation:* User research before launch, keyboard-first versions

**Visual:** Risk matrix with mitigations

**Takeaway:** We've thought through worst-case scenarios and have plans
