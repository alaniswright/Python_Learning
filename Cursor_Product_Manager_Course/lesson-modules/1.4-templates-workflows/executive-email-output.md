This directly impacts our activation OKR and represents ~$24,000 in annual lost revenue.

I recommend implementing a two-part solution: redesign the team invite step to be more prominent with clearer value communication (expected to reduce skip rate from 73% to 50%), and add a 24-hour email reminder for users who skip (expected to recover 20% of solo users).
