Quick update on onboarding research 📊

Just wrapped 12 interviews + 200 survey responses - found that 40% of new users create solo workspaces and never invite teams. These solo users churn at 68% vs 22% for teams. That's ~$4K lost MRR/month.

Recommendation: Redesign invite step (make it more prominent) + 24hr email reminder if skipped. Target: reduce solo rate from 40% to 25%.
