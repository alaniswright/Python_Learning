# TaskFlow Product Update: Workspace Sharing

We're excited to announce Workspace Sharing, a new feature that enables teams to collaborate with external partners, stakeholders, and contractors directly within TaskFlow.

## What is Workspace Sharing?

Workspace Sharing allows you to invite external collaborators to view and contribute to your Workspaces. This feature addresses a critical need for product teams who work with agencies, contractors, and cross-company stakeholders.

## Why This Matters

For product teams like yours, collaboration often extends beyond your immediate team. Whether you're working with design agencies, engineering contractors, or sharing project status with executive stakeholders, Workspace Sharing eliminates the friction of external collaboration.

**Sarah, our Enterprise Admin persona, will appreciate this:** Workspace Sharing includes enterprise-grade access controls, so you can share Workspaces with external partners while maintaining security and compliance. Sarah can set granular permissions, track who has access, and revoke access instantly when needed.

**Mike, our IC Engineer persona, benefits too:** When working with external engineering contractors, Mike can now share specific Workspaces and see their contributions in real-time, without needing to create separate accounts or manage access across multiple systems.

**Alex, our Team Lead persona, will use this for stakeholder visibility:** Alex can share Workspace views with executives and other stakeholders, giving them read-only access to project status without overwhelming them with full Workspace access.

## Key Features

- **Invite external collaborators** via email to specific Workspaces
- **Granular permission controls** - view-only or edit access per Workspace
- **Enterprise security** - SSO support, audit logs, and access tracking
- **Real-time collaboration** - external collaborators see updates instantly with TaskFlow Live
- **Easy access management** - revoke access or change permissions anytime

## How It Works

1. Navigate to your Workspace settings
2. Click "Share Workspace"
3. Enter email addresses of external collaborators
4. Set permission level (view-only or edit)
5. Send invitations

External collaborators receive an email invitation and can access the shared Workspace immediately. They don't need a full TaskFlow account—just access to the Workspaces you've shared.

## Real-World Use Cases

**Design agency collaboration:** Share your product design Workspace with external design agencies. They can view tasks, add design links, and collaborate on design reviews without needing full Workspace access.

**Contractor coordination:** When working with engineering contractors, share specific project Workspaces so they can see context, update task status, and collaborate with your team in real-time.

**Executive visibility:** Share read-only Workspace views with executives and stakeholders. They can see project status and progress without the complexity of full Workspace access.

**Cross-company partnerships:** When collaborating with partner companies on joint initiatives, share relevant Workspaces to coordinate work without creating duplicate project structures.

## Enterprise Benefits

For Enterprise customers like Sarah's organization, Workspace Sharing includes:
- Advanced permission controls at the Workspace level
- Full audit logs of external collaborator activity
- SSO support for external collaborators (when applicable)
- Compliance-ready access tracking and reporting

## Availability

Workspace Sharing is now available for all Pro and Enterprise plans. Free plan members can upgrade to Pro ($12/user/month) to access this feature.

Enterprise customers can enable Workspace Sharing in their Workspace settings. Contact your account manager if you need help configuring external collaborator permissions.

## Getting Started

To start sharing your Workspaces:
1. Go to any Workspace and click "Share" in the top right
2. Enter collaborator email addresses
3. Set permissions (view-only or edit)
4. Send invitations
