## The Solution: TaskFlow Live

TaskFlow Live brings real-time collaboration to project management with features designed for product teams:

### Live Cursors & Presence Indicators

See where teammates are working in real-time. When someone is viewing or editing a task, you'll see their cursor and presence indicator. No more guessing who's working on what.

### Instant Updates

Changes appear immediately for all viewers. No refresh button needed. When a PM updates a PRD, a designer adds a Figma link, or an engineer marks a task complete, everyone sees it instantly.

### Conflict Prevention

Field locking prevents version conflicts. If someone is editing a field, it's automatically locked for others. When they're done, it unlocks. Simple, effective, no conflicts.

### Real-Time Activity Feed

See team activity as it happens. Watch tasks get created, assigned, and completed in real-time. Stay in sync without constant checking.

### Works Everywhere

TaskFlow Live works across web, desktop, and mobile apps. Your team can collaborate whether they're at their desk, on their phone, or working from anywhere.
