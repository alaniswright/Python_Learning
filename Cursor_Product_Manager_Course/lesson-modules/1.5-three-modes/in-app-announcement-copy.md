## Copy Guidelines

**Tone:**
- Friendly and approachable
- Clear and concise
- Action-oriented
- Not overwhelming

**Length:**
- Banners: 1-2 lines max
- Modals: 3-4 short paragraphs
- Tooltips: 1 sentence
- Tour steps: 2-3 sentences

**CTAs:**
- Primary: Enable/Activate/Try
- Secondary: Learn More/Watch Demo
- Tertiary: Maybe Later/Dismiss

**Testing:**
- A/B test different copy variations
- Monitor click-through rates
- Optimize based on user behavior
