## Email Best Practices

**Timing:**
- Enterprise: Tuesday-Thursday, 9-11am (their timezone)
- SMB: Tuesday-Thursday, 10am-2pm (their timezone)

**Personalization:**
- Use customer name and company name
- Reference specific pain points from previous conversations
- Include relevant use cases for their industry

**CTAs:**
- Primary CTA: Enable/Activate TaskFlow Live
- Secondary CTA: Schedule Demo/View Documentation
- Keep CTAs clear and action-oriented

**Testing:**
- A/B test subject lines
- Test different CTA copy
- Monitor open/click rates and optimize
